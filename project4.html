Mobile Game / 2023

MindMaze.

A two-player memory game where players take turns matching pairs, collecting points, and competing to finish with the highest score.

PROJECTMobile memory game
PLAYERSTwo players, taking turns
MODES4 × 4 · 4 × 5 · 4 × 6

01 / Choose your challenge

Three ways to play.

A soft, light-coloured interface helps make the memory game comfortable to use. Players can choose a 4 × 4, 4 × 5, or 4 × 6 board, adding variety and different levels of challenge.

A mode for every mood and memory.

02 / Two-player turns

Remember. Match. Score.

Players take turns uncovering cards and earn points for successful matches. Scores stay visible in the top corners, and the active-player label—P1 or P2—makes each turn clear. The player with the highest score when the board is complete wins.

Take turns and keep an eye on the score.

03 / A growing challenge

More cards. More to remember.

Boards grow from 4 × 4 to 4 × 5 and 4 × 6, making each mode progressively more complex. Turn indicators distinguish the active player from the waiting player, so both people can follow the round.

Increasing board sizes add a fresh challenge.

04 / Game results

A winner—and what’s next.

Once all pairs have been found, a Game Over message announces the final scores and winner. Players can start a new round or exit to the main screen and choose another mode.

Celebrate the win or jump into another round.

Ready for another round?
